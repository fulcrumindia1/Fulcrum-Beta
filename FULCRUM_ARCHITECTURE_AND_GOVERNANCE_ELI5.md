## 🏛️ The Big Picture: The "Hospital & School" Analogy

To understand Fulcrum without any tech jargon, imagine a **Specialized Entrepreneurship Hospital & Academy**:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                              DIRECTORATE / ADMIN                                |
|          "The Chief Medical Superintendent / School Principal"                  |
|  - Sees the whole hospital.                                                     |
|  - Assigns doctors to patients.                                                 |
|  - Disciplines staff, grants doctor licenses, and reads confidential feedback.  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
       |                                                    |
       v                                                    v
+-------------------------------+              +----------------------------------+
|        PRIMARY GUIDES         |              |           DOMAIN SMEs            |
| "Personal Family Doctor"      |              | "Specialist Surgeon / Expert"    |
| - Dedicated 1-on-1 to founder.|              | - Called in only for hard topics |
| - Holds patient's hand.       |              |   (e.g., Tax, Patents, FSSAI).   |
| - Vets medicine (schemes).    |              | - Does NOT manage day-to-day.    |
+-------------------------------+              +----------------------------------+
       \                                            /
        \                                          /
         v                                        v
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             ASPIRANT (THE FOUNDER)                              |
|                           "The Patient / Student"                               |
| - Wants to build or grow a business (e.g. Coir workshop in Kanyakumari).       |
| - Does NOT see hospital administration, doctor salary logs, or medical vaults.  |
| - Only receives verified medicine and direct advice from their assigned Guide.  |
```

## 👤 Meet Our Running Real-World Example
Throughout this guide, we follow **Mathew**:
* **Who he is:** A 28-year-old entrepreneur from **Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu**.
* **His Business:** *Mathew's Agro Coir Works* (turning coconut husks into export-grade plant pots and geotextile mats).
* **His Need:** Needs ₹25 Lakhs for machinery and help getting an export license and MSME subsidy.
* **His Assigned Guide:** **Priya** (an experienced industrial mentor in Madurai).
* **His Assigned SME:** **Dr. Arumugam** (a Chennai-based Export & GST Tax Advocate).
* **The Administrator:** **Fulcrum Directorate** (`fulcrumindia1@gmail.com`).

## 🗂️ The 11 Admin Tabs — ELI5 Breakdown

Here is what every single tab in the Admin Control Tower does, how it connects to Mathew, Priya, and Dr. Arumugam, and where the secret walls (disconnections) are.

---

### Tab 1: 👥 Aspirants & Journeys
> **ELI5:** *The Master Patient Record & Medical Chart.*

#### Real-World Example:
Admin opens Tab 1 and clicks on **Mathew**. Admin instantly sees Mathew's face photo, his factory address in Kanyakumari, his ₹25 Lakh machinery funding requirement, who his mentor is, and what milestones he has achieved so far. If a bank officer asks for his official dossier, Admin clicks **"Download Official PDF Dossier"** and hands over an 8-page verified document.

* **What Mathew Sees:** In his **"Overview"** and **"My Journey"** tabs, Mathew sees his own roadmap (e.g. *"Machinery Ordered"*, *"GST Registered"*).
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** Priya sees Mathew's full roadmap in her **"My Aspirants"** workspace and can add verified milestone stamps.
* **What SME Dr. Arumugam Sees:** Read-only access to Mathew's business profile when answering a tax ticket.
* **What Admin Sees:** Every single founder in Tamil Nadu in one searchable directory, their completion percentage, and full history.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Updates made by Mathew in Section 5 or by Priya in the Journey update the Admin Dossier and the downloadable PDF in real time.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Mathew **cannot** see other founders' profiles or journeys. Complete data isolation.

---

### Tab 2: 🧭 Guides Management
> **ELI5:** *The Doctor's Staff Room & Roster.*

#### Real-World Example:
Admin reviews Guide **Priya's** profile. Admin sees Priya currently mentors 4 founders (including Mathew), her specialty is *Manufacturing & Agro-processing*, and her contact details. If Priya takes a sabbatical, Admin can mark her inactive so no new founders are assigned to her.

* **What Mathew Sees:** Nothing. Mathew only knows Priya is his guide. He cannot see other guides in the directory.
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** In her **"My Profile"** tab, she sees her own bio, specialties, and assigned mentee count.
* **What SME Dr. Arumugam Sees:** Nothing about guide administration.
* **What Admin Sees:** A complete table of all certified Guides, their active caseloads, phone numbers, and operational districts.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** When a Guide updates their phone or bio, it instantly updates the mentorship card inside Mathew's *"My Mentors"* tab.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Guides **cannot** see each other's caseload notes or private conversations.

---

### Tab 3: 🔬 SMEs Management
> **ELI5:** *The Specialist Surgeon On-Call Directory.*

#### Real-World Example:
Mathew needs to know how to claim a 5% GST refund on exported coir mats. A regular Guide might not know high-level tax law. Admin goes to Tab 3 and maintains the roster of verified **Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)** — like **Dr. Arumugam** (Taxation) or **Advocate Suresh** (Patents & Trademark).

* **What Mathew Sees:** Mathew sees Dr. Arumugam listed in his *"Guidance Team"* once Admin attaches him to Mathew's ticket.
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** Priya can see which SMEs are available so she can request the Admin to bring Dr. Arumugam into Mathew's case.
* **What SME Dr. Arumugam Sees:** His own profile, areas of expertise, and past resolved legal/tax tickets.
* **What Admin Sees:** The full directory of domain specialists, their verified certifications, and how many tickets they have answered.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** When Admin links an SME, that SME's name appears in Mathew's consultation dropdown.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** SMEs **cannot** release government schemes or edit Mathew's general journey. They only give specialized technical advice.

---

### Tab 4: 🎯 Mentor Assignments
> **ELI5:** *The Matchmaker & Emergency Handover Desk.*

#### Real-World Example:
Mathew signs up. He is all alone. Admin goes to Tab 4:
1. Admin selects **Mathew** $\rightarrow$ selects **Guide Priya** $\rightarrow$ clicks **"Assign Guide"**.
2. **What if Priya gets sick or stops replying?** Admin opens Mathew's card, selects **Guide B (Karthik)** from the *"Replace with"* dropdown, and clicks **"Confirm Replace"**.
3. **Magic happens behind the scenes:** All of Mathew's open help questions are smoothly moved to Karthik's desk, and Priya is quietly unassigned.

* **What Mathew Sees:** In *"My Mentors"*, his primary mentor card changes from Priya to Karthik with a friendly notification.
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** Mathew disappears from her active caseload.
* **What New Guide Karthik Sees:** Mathew immediately appears in his *"My Aspirants"* list with all past notes preserved.
* **What Admin Sees:** Live visual cards of all pairings, with one-click Unassign or Replace buttons.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Every change writes an indelible record into `assignment_history` for government auditing.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Founders **cannot** assign themselves a guide or pick whoever they want. Only Admin holds the authority to assign mentors to prevent favoritism.

---

### Tab 5: 🏛️ Mentor Inquiries & Directives
> **ELI5:** *The Red Phone Between Mentor and Government Directorate.*

#### Real-World Example:
Guide Priya is helping Mathew apply for the **PMEGP Scheme**. But the local branch bank manager in Kanyakumari is illegally refusing to accept the application without collateral. 
* Priya cannot fight the bank alone. 
* She goes to her Guide Portal and clicks **"Submit Roadblock to Directorate"**.
* Admin sees it in Tab 5, contacts the State Lead Bank Manager, issues an official **Administrative Directive**, and attaches it to Priya's file.

* **What Mathew Sees:** **NOTHING.** (Mathew is protected from bureaucratic panic; his mentors handle institutional friction behind the scenes).
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** A private communication line to the Admin where she receives official backing and binding directives.
* **What SME Dr. Arumugam Sees:** Can be tagged into the discussion if legal action against the bank is needed.
* **What Admin Sees:** An inbox of serious ground-level roadblocks across all districts in Tamil Nadu.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** When Admin types an answer, Priya gets an immediate in-app ping in her Guide Portal.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Strictly invisible to the Aspirant. The founder never sees internal mentor-to-admin arguments or complaints about bank bureaucracy.

---

### Tab 6: 🏦 Scheme Catalogue (CRUD)
> **ELI5:** *The 179-Medicine Central Pharmacy Vault.*

#### Real-World Example:
The Central Government launches a brand new subsidy: *"PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana"*. Admin opens Tab 6, clicks **"Add New Scheme"**, types in the subsidy amount, eligibility (rooftop solar, MSME category), and application link. It is added to the 179 master database.

* **What Mathew Sees:** **NOTHING directly from this catalogue!** Mathew CANNOT browse this 179-scheme library. (Why? Because if a founder sees 179 schemes, they get overwhelmed, apply for the wrong grants, and get rejected).
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** Priya also cannot see the raw catalogue. The AI matching engine scans the catalogue against Mathew's profile and shows Priya only the top scored schemes ($\ge$60%).
* **What Admin Sees:** Complete CRUD control (Create, Read, Update, Delete, Archive) over all 179 Central & State schemes.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** When Admin adds or edits a scheme, the AI Matching Engine immediately includes it in the next scan for all founders.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Direct access to this library is blocked for Aspirants and Guides. Only vetted, released schemes reach the founder.

---

### Tab 7: 🔑 Multi-Role Grants
> **ELI5:** *Promoting a Star Student to Teacher's Assistant.*

#### Real-World Example:
Mathew's coir business becomes wildly successful after 3 years. He is now an industry legend in Kanyakumari. A new young founder joins who wants to make coir ropes. Admin wants Mathew to mentor this new founder. Admin goes to Tab 7, finds Mathew, and clicks **"Grant Guide Role"**.

* **What Mathew Sees:** Mathew logs in. In his sidebar, he now has a **Role Switcher Dropdown**:
  - He can switch to **"Aspirant View"** to manage his own business.
  - He can switch to **"Guide View"** to mentor younger entrepreneurs!
* **The Iron-Clad Security Rule (Self-Assignment Wall):** Mathew **CANNOT** be his own Guide. The system strictly checks `aspirant_id != guide_id`. Mathew still has Guide Priya for his own venture.
* **What Admin Sees:** Complete control to grant or revoke secondary roles (`aspirant`, `guide`, `sme`).
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Multi-role permissions are stored in `user_roles` and checked on every page load.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** A user cannot promote themselves. Only the Admin email (`fulcrumindia1@gmail.com`) can grant roles.

---

### Tab 8: 📜 Legal & NDA Records
> **ELI5:** *The Digital Notary & Non-Disclosure Gatekeeper.*

#### Real-World Example:
Before Guide Priya or SME Dr. Arumugam can see Mathew's secret coir manufacturing formulas or turnover numbers, they MUST legally promise not to steal his trade secrets. When they log in, a full-screen window locks their screen: *"You must accept the Mentor NDA & Code of Conduct"*. They type their full name to sign.

* **What Mathew, Priya, and Dr. Arumugam Experience:** If they haven't signed, they cannot click anything. Once signed, the gate unlocks.
* **What Admin Sees in Tab 8:** A legal courtroom-ready audit log:
  - *"Priya S accepted NDA on 2026-11-04 10:15 UTC from IP 157.44.x.x"*
  - *"Mathew accepted Platform Terms on 2026-11-02 08:30 UTC"*
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Checked automatically at login (`check_user_acceptance`).
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Users cannot delete or tamper with their signature records.

---

### Tab 9: 🛡️ Confidential Complaints
> **ELI5:** *The Whistleblower / Anti-Harassment Secret Drop Box.*

#### Real-World Example:
What if Guide Priya stops caring, speaks rudely, or asks Mathew for private consulting fees under the table? Mathew cannot complain to Priya directly.
* Mathew goes to his portal under *"My Mentors"* $\rightarrow$ clicks **"Request Guide Change / Report Confidential Concern"**.
* He types: *"Guide is asking for 5% commission on my loan."*
* He clicks submit.

* **What Mathew Sees:** A reassuring message: *"Your confidential concern has been transmitted directly to the Program Directorate."*
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** **ABSOLUTELY NOTHING.** Priya has zero knowledge that a report was filed.
* **What Admin Sees in Tab 9:** A red alert with Mathew's full report, the Guide's name, timestamp, and action buttons (*"Caution Guide"*, *"Reassign Founder"*, *"Suspend Guide"*).
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Written to `guide_complaints` table with an Admin-only query policy.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** 100% invisible to the Guide and SME. Total whistleblower protection.

---

### Tab 10: ⏱️ Inactivity Monitor
> **ELI5:** *The Mentor Attendance & Laziness Alarm.*

#### Real-World Example:
Guide Priya gets busy and forgets to check in on Mathew for 10 days. Mathew has an open question waiting.
* **How the platform catches her:** The platform scans every pair every day. It checks:
  1. Did Priya answer Mathew's consultation questions?
  2. Did Priya release any new schemes to Mathew?
  3. Did Priya log any journey milestones for Mathew?
* **The Escalation Ladder:**
  - **Day 7:** System sends a polite notification to Priya: *"Action Recommended: You haven't checked in with Mathew in 7 days."*
  - **Day 10:** Logged in Admin Tab 10 as an official warning.
  - **Day 14:** Turns **Bright Red** on the Admin dashboard: *"CRITICAL: 14+ Days Inactivity. Recommend reassigning Mathew to another mentor."*
* **How Priya resets the clock:** The moment Priya types a reply to Mathew, releases a scheme, or logs a milestone, **the clock resets to 0 Days!**

* **What Mathew Sees:** Mathew doesn't see the alarm clock; he just gets a fast reply from his mentor.
* **What Guide Priya Sees:** A reminder in her notification bell if she neglects a founder.
* **What Admin Sees in Tab 10:** A real-time leaderboard of all quiet or delinquent mentors, with a *"Run Caseload Inactivity Scan Now"* button.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Directly wired to `help_requests`, `scheme_releases`, and `journey_events`.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** The founder does not see the Guide's warning strikes.

---

### Tab 11: ⚖️ SME Conflict Governance
> **ELI5:** *The Insider Trading & Commercial Conflict Shield.*

#### Real-World Example:
SME **Dr. Arumugam** is assigned to give tax advice to Mathew's coir factory. But secretly, Dr. Arumugam's brother owns a competing coir factory in the next town! 
* Under strict ethics rules, Dr. Arumugam must declare this.
* Dr. Arumugam goes to his portal $\rightarrow$ clicks **"Declare Conflict of Interest"** $\rightarrow$ selects Mathew's enterprise $\rightarrow$ explains the family connection.
* Admin sees it in Tab 11, clicks **"Approve & Re-route"**, and assigns a neutral SME to Mathew instead.

* **What Mathew Sees:** His case is handled with 100% integrity without his trade secrets leaking to a competitor.
* **What SME Dr. Arumugam Sees:** A safe, legal way to recuse himself from cases where he has a conflict.
* **What Admin Sees:** A complete audit log of all declared conflicts, protecting the Directorate from legal lawsuits.
* **🔗 Connected Wire:** Prevents the conflicted SME from opening Mathew's financial files.
* **🚫 Firewalled (Not Connected):** Conflicted SMEs are locked out of the founder's documents.

## 🔄 The Master Connection vs. Disconnection Table

| Feature / Wire | Is it Connected? | How it Works (Grassroots Level) | Why it is DISCONNECTED (The Firewall) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Aspirant Profile $\rightarrow$ Matching Engine** | **YES** | Mathew enters his ₹25L machine need $\rightarrow$ AI calculates high-probability schemes. | Mathew CANNOT see raw matching algorithms or unvetted results. |
| **Matching Engine $\rightarrow$ Guide Review** | **YES** | Priya sees schemes that scored $\ge$60% with match reasons. | Priya CANNOT see schemes that scored below threshold or other guides' candidates. |
| **Guide $\rightarrow$ Aspirant Scheme Tab** | **YES** | Priya clicks "Release Scheme" $\rightarrow$ Scheme pops up in Mathew's portal. | Mathew NEVER sees the 179-scheme repository; strictly sees only released ones. |
| **Aspirant Help Ticket $\rightarrow$ Guide Desk** | **YES** | Mathew types a question $\rightarrow$ Priya receives it in her Consultations tab. | Tickets do NOT leak to other guides or unrelated founders. |
| **Guide Response $\rightarrow$ Inactivity Clock** | **YES** | Priya hits "Submit Guidance" $\rightarrow$ Inactivity clock resets to **0 days**. | Aspirant does NOT see the inactivity timer or internal warning strikes. |
| **Confidential Complaint $\rightarrow$ Admin** | **YES** | Mathew reports mentor misbehavior $\rightarrow$ Goes straight to Admin Tab 9. | **STRICTLY BLOCKED FROM GUIDE.** The Guide has zero visibility of the complaint. |
| **Mentor Roadblock $\rightarrow$ Admin Directive** | **YES** | Priya reports corrupt bank officer $\rightarrow$ Admin issues legal directive in Tab 5. | **STRICTLY BLOCKED FROM ASPIRANT.** Prevents founder panic and confusion. |
| **Admin Reassignment $\rightarrow$ Ticket Transfer** | **YES** | Admin replaces Priya with Karthik $\rightarrow$ Open tickets automatically move to Karthik. | Priya loses all access to Mathew's private discussions immediately. |
| **Multi-Role Promotion $\rightarrow$ Self-Assignment** | **YES** | Mathew is promoted to Guide $\rightarrow$ can mentor others via sidebar switch. | **SELF-MENTORING BLOCKED (`asp != guide`).** Mathew cannot approve his own schemes. |
| **SME Conflict Declaration $\rightarrow$ Access Revocation** | **YES** | SME reports conflict $\rightarrow$ Admin re-routes $\rightarrow$ SME's access to founder is cut. | Conflicted SME cannot read financial balance sheets or trade secrets. |

## 💡 Summary in 3 Golden Rules:

1. **The Aspirant is Protected:** The founder never suffers from information overload (179 schemes), internal bureaucratic fights, or mentor conflicts. They get a focused, clean path to grow their enterprise.
2. **The Guide is Supported & Monitored:** The Guide has AI to pick the right schemes and Admin backing to solve roadblocks, but is held strictly accountable by the 7/10/14-day inactivity clock.
3. **The Admin is the Supreme Custodian:** The single Admin holds total visibility, controls mentor allocations, enforces legal NDAs, resolves conflicts of interest, and safeguards the integrity of the entire ecosystem.
